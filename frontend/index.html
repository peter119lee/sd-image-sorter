Caption editor
What makes a good caption?

For LoRA training each .txt should describe what's in the image with a few short tags. The trainer learns to associate your trigger word with everything those tags share across images.

  • DO include character / subject features (hair color, clothes, pose, scene).
  • DO include your trigger word once (use Workbench "Common tags" so it lands in every caption).
  • DON'T describe things that are constant across all images — that's what your trigger word is for.
  • DON'T leave .txt files empty — empty captions teach the model nothing.

Shortcut: click "Tag all images" in Step A and you're done — the WD14 tagger writes a perfectly serviceable starter caption per image.
